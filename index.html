Van or coach?

Van, clearly

The coach fare alone looks cheap. The problem is the last 30 km: Ohiya is not on the NCG route, and a Friday night coach drops you at Haputale in the small hours with eleven people, camping gear, and no way to reach a gate that opens at 6 am.

What to pin down before you pay a deposit

Local van owners quote one of two ways. Get the quote in writing either way, because the difference between them on a 450 km trip is real money.

Per-kilometre hire
Rs 100–150 per km for a KDH with driver, fuel included, with a minimum of 100–150 km charged per day whether you drive it or not. Rs 110/km is a fair local rate to aim for.
Day package
Rs 15,000–25,000 per day, which typically includes 100–150 km. Anything beyond that is billed as excess.
Excess kilometres
Rs 60–150 per km depending on the operator. Tourist-facing firms bill around USD 0.43, roughly Rs 130. Ask for the number before you sign, not after.
How km are counted
Most owners count garage to garage, not from your pickup point. On a Colombo–Ohiya round trip that can add 30–50 km. Ask which it is.
Driver allowance
Rs 2,500–4,000 per night for meals and a place to sleep on Saturday, plus about Rs 1,000 night parking. A night-driving surcharge applies between 8 pm and 5 am, which covers almost the whole Friday run — get it quoted as a figure, not a vague extra.
The three-day minimum
This hire touches three calendar days, so a 150 km-per-day minimum clause means 450 km billed whether you drive it or not. That happens to match the real distance here, but confirm you are billed on actual kilometres, otherwise a shorter route saves you nothing.
If you use a member's own van
About 450 km at roughly 10 km per litre, with auto diesel at Rs 407, comes to around Rs 18,300 in fuel. Cheaper on paper, but somebody has to drive up a hill road at 3 am and then not hike.
Park vehicle entry
Rs 250–1,000 at the Horton Plains gate depending on the vehicle. Budget Rs 750 for a van and carry cash.

Friday night to Sunday

The hike lands on Saturday morning

Driving through the night puts you at the gate for the 6 am opening with the whole day in hand, which is what Kirigalpotta needs — 14 km return, six to seven hours, and mist that closes in by mid-morning in October. The cost is that nobody will have slept properly before a hard hike. Plan the van seats for sleeping and keep Friday night quiet.

Before you collect money

Seven things to settle

Does Rs 9,400 buy the site or eight beds?

The site is booked and yours from Saturday evening. The only thing left to confirm is whether that figure covers the pitch regardless of headcount, or eight sleeping spots with three people unaccounted for. Ask the operator for the tent count in writing, then zero out the top-up line or leave it in.

No polythene past the gate

Horton Plains is a strict plastic-free zone and bags are checked on the way in. Decant water into reusable bottles, repack noodles and snacks into tins or cloth bags, and carry every wrapper back out — there are no bins on the trail.

Beer stays at the camp

Alcohol and smoking are banned inside the park, with spot fines and removal for anyone caught. The drinks budget is for the Ohiya campsite on night one only.

Take a tracker for Kirigalpotta

There is no separate permit — the park ticket covers the trail. But it is unmarked, groups do get lost on the way down, and eleven people spread across a bog is exactly how that happens. Budget for a park tracker or guide.

October is the wet half of the year

The clear season at Horton Plains is January to March. Early October sits in the second inter-monsoon: showers, mist by mid-morning, slippery rock and leeches on the Kirigalpotta trail. Pack rain gear and leech socks as standard, not as optional, and accept that the summit view is a coin toss.

Nobody will have slept

A six-hour overnight drive followed straight by a seven-hour hike is the real risk in this plan. Eat properly on Friday evening, keep the van quiet after Balangoda, and set the 10 am turnaround so a tired group is not still descending in afternoon mist.

Book the van this week

The 3rd and 4th are a Saturday and Sunday, three weeks out. Good KDH owners are taken early for weekends, and you need one who will do a 10:30 pm departure without inflating the night surcharge.

Everything rides in the van on Saturday

Because the site opens Saturday evening, tents and cooking kit spend the whole hiking day in the van at the park gate. Pack so that what you need on the trail is in daypacks and nothing valuable is left loose in the vehicle.
